Our Oceans: A World of Wonder

The ocean covers more than two-thirds of our planet and is home to an extraordinary variety of life. From colourful coral reefs to the darkest deep-sea valleys, every part of the ocean has its own fascinating world.

Dolphins travel in lively groups, sea turtles cross entire oceans, and tiny plankton support food chains that help keep marine ecosystems healthy. These creatures all play an important role in the balance of nature.

Life Beneath the Surface

Coral reefs are among the richest habitats on Earth. They provide food and shelter for thousands of species, while mangrove forests and seagrass beds protect coastlines and serve as nurseries for young marine animals.

Healthy oceans also help regulate the climate, produce much of the oxygen we breathe, and support the lives and livelihoods of communities around the world.

Keep the Blue Planet Blue

Plastic pollution, overfishing, and rising ocean temperatures are serious challenges. We can all help by reducing single-use plastic, choosing sustainable seafood, and supporting efforts to protect marine habitats. Small actions, when shared by many people, can make a meaningful difference.
