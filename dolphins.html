The ocean is a vast and fascinating world that covers most of our planet. It is home to an incredible variety of living things, from tiny drifting plankton to the largest animals on Earth. Every habitat plays a part in keeping the marine environment healthy.

Coral reefs are often called the rainforests of the sea. Their colourful structures provide food and shelter for many fish and other creatures. Reefs also help protect coastlines from strong waves.

Sea turtles travel across wide stretches of ocean and return to sandy beaches to lay their eggs.

Dolphins are intelligent, social mammals that communicate through clicks, whistles, and body movements.

Far below the sunlight, deep-sea animals survive in cold, dark water. Scientists continue to discover new species and learn how these unusual ecosystems work.

We can help protect the ocean by reducing plastic waste, avoiding litter on beaches, and supporting responsible fishing. Healthy oceans are important for wildlife and people everywhere.
